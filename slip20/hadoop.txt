a. Create input and output directories in HDFS.
hdfs dfs -mkdir /input
hdfs dfs -mkdir /output


b. Upload data.csv to the input directory.
hdfs dfs -put data.csv /input/


c. Copy data.csv to the output directory.
hdfs dfs -cp /input/data.csv /output/


d. Rename data.csv to processed_data.csv in the output directory.
hdfs dfs -mv /output/data.csv /output/processed_data.csv
